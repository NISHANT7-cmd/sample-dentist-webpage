About Green Cross

Established with a vision to redefine healthcare in the islands, Green Cross Health Care combines cutting-edge medical technology with the warmth of personalized care. We believe that everyone deserves access to world-class medical services regardless of their location.

Our facility is equipped with advanced diagnostic tools and staffed by specialists from across the nation, ensuring that complex medical needs are met right here in the heart of our community.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment? expand_more
You can book an appointment through our website using the "Book Appointment" button, by calling our 24/7 helpline, or by visiting our reception desk in person.
What are the emergency contact numbers? expand_more
For immediate emergency assistance, please call our 24/7 ER hotline at +91 123 456 7890. Our ambulance services are available throughout the island.
Do you accept medical insurance? expand_more
Yes, we are empaneled with most major government and private health insurance providers. Please check with our TPA desk for specific coverage details.
